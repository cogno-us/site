Why agent governance belongs in a control plane, not a log

Logging describes what an agent did. Observability describes how the system behaved. Neither answers whether an action was permitted before it executed. This note works through the boundary between the three, and why the decision point has to sit in the execution path rather than beside it.

Andre de Lima · Founder & Architect
