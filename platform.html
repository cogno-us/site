The platform beneath enterprise AI — powered by ISS and Navalia.

The Cognous Platform is where AI work is executed, optimized, governed, and delivered. Two working components carry it: the ISS substrate, which makes reasoning stable and economical beneath any model, and Navalia, which turns raw signal into verified, evidence-linked intelligence. Both run on the same governance spine.

One layer between your applications and the models.

What the platform provides

Six governance surfaces, present at every step.

These are not features bolted onto output. They are the conditions under which AI work is allowed to proceed.

I
Identity

Every action is attributable to an agent, a delegating human, and the authority under which it ran.

II
Authority scope

What an agent may propose is declared in advance and evaluated before execution, not reviewed afterward.

III
State

Working context persists across calls, so the system stops rebuilding what it already knew.

IV
Lineage

Each result is traceable to the step that produced it and the constraints in force at the time.

V
Inspection

Reasoning is recorded as inspectable state changes, not an opaque completion.

VI
Certification

Certified, checked, refused, revoked — deterministic refusal is a primitive.
